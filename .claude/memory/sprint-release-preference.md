After Sprint 7 (Admin/Audit), stop adding functionality and dedicate a **Release Candidate (RC) sprint** to polishing and bug fixing. Many projects skip this; it has a huge impact on how professional the final product feels.

Sprint order:
1. ✅ Platform Foundation
2. ✅ Device Management
3. ✅ Dashboard
4. ✅ Alerts
5. ✅ Event History
6. ✅ Reports
7. Sprint 6 — Users / Roles / Permissions
8. Sprint 7 — Admin Settings / Audit Logs
9. Final RC v1.0 Polish (accessibility, performance, loading states, animations, empty states, documentation)

**Why:** The user wants a coherent v1.0, not an endless series of new features. The platform foundation is sufficient; move to admin features, then ship.

**How to apply:** After Sprint 7, do not expand the roadmap with new feature sprints. Switch to a dedicated RC sprint for polish and bug fixes before declaring v1.0.
